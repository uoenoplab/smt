SMT on the Wire

How SMT turns one Homa message into authenticated TLS records and packets, and back. The bytes are real: your browser runs AES-GCM with the fixed test key of unloaded/.

Sizes are shrunk so that the bytes fit on the screen: here a segment holds 16 message bytes and a record 3 segments. In SMT a record holds up to max_gso_data bytes (at most 16 KB, 20 segments). The layout, the header sizes, the sequence number, the nonce and the AAD follow smt_outgoing.c, smt_sw.c and smt_impl.h.
